Your Rights

Welcome to Flight Rights NZ!

I set up this website so people could learn about their rights when airlines in NZ delay or cancel their flights.

Most people don't realise when your flight is delayed you can be reimbursed for the costs of the delay, which include food, drink, accommodation, transportation and even booking a separate flight with a separate airline.

The limits set by companies like Jetstar are arbitrary and designed to convince unsuspecting consumers that they are only owed what is offered by the airline, when in fact the amount is much higher, up to 10 times the cost of your ticket.

Click on the Your Rights tab to learn more about your rights when a flight has been delayed or cancelled.

If you would like to claim reimbursement from an airline, simply fill in the details of your flight in the form below, and an automated letter will be generated that you can email to the airline, along with evidence of costs, such as receipts or a screenshot of your bank statement, requesting compensation.
